The context-free grammar for this calculator is as follows:

<expression> ::= <additive>

<additive> ::= <additive> "+" <multiplicative> 
             | <additive> "-" <multiplicative> 
             | <multiplicative>

<multiplicative> ::= <multiplicative> "*" <unary> 
                   | <multiplicative> "/" <unary> 
                   | <multiplicative> "mod" <unary> 
                   | <unary>

<unary> ::= "-" <unary> 
          | "+" <unary> 
          | <power>

<power> ::= <primary> "^" <unary> 
          | <primary>

<primary> ::= <number> 
            | "(" <expression> ")" 
            | <function> 
            | <variable>

<function> ::= <function_name> "(" <expression> ")"


<variable> ::= <first_letter> <alnums> 
             | <first_letter>

<alnums> ::= <alnum> <alnums> 
           | <alnum>

<alnum> ::= <letter> <alnum> 
          | <digit> <alnum> 
          | <letter> 
          | <digit>

<number> ::= "pi" | "e" | <decimal>

<decimal> ::= <digits> 
            | <digits> "." 
            | "." <digits> 
            | <digits> "." <digits>

<digits> ::= <digit> <digits> 
           | <digit>

<function_name> ::= "sqrt" | "sin" | "cos" | ... (please check calculator.c for full list)

<first_letter> ::= "_" | <letter>

<letter> ::= "a" | ... | "z" | "A" | ... | "Z"

<digit> ::= "0" | "1" | "2" | ... | "9"

The REPL extension grammar is as follows:

<REPL> ::= "var " <variable> "=" <expression>
         | <expression>
         | "del " <variable>
         | "exit"
         | "help"

Note that the suffixed spaces matter in the strings.

Example with calculator CFG: Generate -sin(pi/2) * 2

<expression>
-> <additive>
-> <multiplicative>
-> <multiplicative> "*" <unary>
-> <unary> "*" <unary>
-> "-" <unary> "*" <unary>
-> "-" <power> "*" <unary>
-> "-" <primary> "*" <unary>
-> "-" <function> "*" <unary>
-> "-" <function_name> "(" <expression> ")*" <unary>
-> "-sin(" <expression> ")*" <unary>
-> "-sin(" <additive> ")*" <unary>
-> "-sin(" <multiplicative> ")*" <unary>
-> "-sin(" <multiplicative> "/" <unary> ")*" <unary>
-> "-sin(" <unary> "/" <unary> ")*" <unary>
-> "-sin(" <power> "/" <unary> ")*" <unary>
-> "-sin(" <primary> "/" <unary> ")*" <unary>
-> "-sin(" <number> "/" <unary> ")*" <unary>
-> "-sin(pi/" <unary> ")*" <unary>
-> "-sin(pi/" <power> ")*" <unary>
-> "-sin(pi/" <primary> ")*" <unary>
-> "-sin(pi/" <number> ")*" <unary>
-> "-sin(pi/" <decimal> ")*" <unary>
-> "-sin(pi/" <digits> ")*" <unary>
-> "-sin(pi/" <digit> ")*" <unary>
-> "-sin(pi/2)*" <unary>
-> "-sin(pi/2)*" <power>
-> "-sin(pi/2)*" <primary>
-> "-sin(pi/2)*" <number>
-> "-sin(pi/2)*" <decimal>
-> "-sin(pi/2)*" <digits>
-> "-sin(pi/2)*" <digit>
-> "-sin(pi/2)*2"

Feel free to verify this grammar for yourself.
